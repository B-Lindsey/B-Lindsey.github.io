CrossFire PRO Plasma Procedures

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Personal notes, unlisted. I’m Bernie Lindsey, and I’ve run my CrossFire PRO since August 2024. These are my notes, put together with help from an AI research assistant that read Langmuir and Razorweld documents and Langmuir forum posts. Where a page says “I” or “my”, it means me and my machine. Most of this is compiled research I haven’t tried yet; each page has a status line saying which. Not affiliated with Langmuir Systems, Razorweld, or Jasic. Follow the manufacturers’ manuals, safety instructions, and support advice over anything here. More in About this site, including a glossary.

My setup
#

  • Table: Langmuir CrossFire PRO, 48 x 33 in cut area, ACME lead screws, IHS standard, Langmuir THC added. Electronics on 120 V, in an enclosure wall-mounted away from the table.
  • Cutter: Razorweld CUT45 CNC (model JRWPC45LH; Langmuir sells it as the “Razorweld Razorcut 45 with Machine Torch”) on 230 V (20–45 A), X45 machine torch in the Machine Torch Mount.
  • Air: Ingersoll-Rand SS3L3 60-gallon compressor → IR D18IN refrigerated dryer → Harbor Freight filter → cutter.
  • Software: Inkscape and Fusion (design), SheetCAM 8.0 with the FireControl post v1.6 (CAM), FireControl (machine control).
  • Materials: mild steel, stainless, and aluminum, mostly 3/16 in and thinner.
  • Shop: a home shop.

How to read these pages
#

  • Status line. Every procedure page starts with one of: Tested on my machine (nothing yet), Used by me, not formally tested, or Compiled research, not yet tried. The FireControl scenario pages (FC-02 to FC-07) are marked Reference: compiled from Langmuir docs and forum threads, and the test-cut pages are marked planned.
  • How sources are weighted: Langmuir’s own documents and staff first, then the Razorweld/Jasic manuals (my printed JRWPC45LH manual first; the online Razorcut 45 DI manual is for a related but different model), then experienced forum members, then everyone else. Every number says where it came from. Numbers from members with a different plasma cutter are only a rough guide for the CUT45.
  • Cut charts. I couldn’t find a published X45 machine-torch cut chart (as of October 2026). My starting chart came from Razorweld: they sent it to forum member MO-Hacker, who posted it (https://forum.langmuirsystems.com/t/847/29). Because it’s manufacturer data, it’s weighted just below Langmuir’s own documents. I’ve cut with these settings and they worked well, but they haven’t been formally tested yet. A second candidate is a T45m-format chart (laid out like Hypertherm’s Powermax45 charts; T45m is Hypertherm’s Powermax45 machine torch) that FSFab says Jasic supplied (https://forum.langmuirsystems.com/t/45011; origin unconfirmed). Its arc voltages (111–123 V) disagree with the Razorweld chart’s (139–152 V). The test-cut program is how I plan to settle that.
  • Safety items (house wiring, grounding the supply, fumes from coated or galvanized stock) go to the manufacturers’ documents. See https://www.langmuirsystems.com/pages/safety and the printed operating manual that came with my cutter (JRWPC45LH).
  • Test results go in the test-cut log. It has planned rows for test sessions S01 (16 ga mild steel), S04 (3/16 in mild steel), and the optional S04B (3/16 in mild steel, T45m-format chart at 45 A vs. Razorweld chart at 40 A). Results then go into each page’s “Bernie’s test results” section.

FireControl

  • FC-02: Scenario: FireControl won’t open (“Child process exited with code 1”). The most-viewed FireControl problem on the forum. Reference: compiled from Langmuir docs and forum threads.
  • FC-03: Scenario: FireControl won’t connect: CrossFire greyed out, or only a COM port shows. Reference: compiled from Langmuir docs and forum threads.
  • FC-04: Scenario: connection lost or frozen mid-cut, computer side. Check EMI in grounding first. Reference: compiled from Langmuir docs and forum threads.
  • FC-05: Scenario: resuming a cut after a stop: Resume, Run From Loop or Line, and M00 pauses. Reference: compiled from Langmuir docs and forum threads.

Quick-start values (Langmuir SheetCAM guide example tool for a Razorweld 45A cutter on the PRO; test before trusting)
#

Pierce height 0.15 in · pierce delay 0.5 s · plunge 50 ipm · cut height 0.08 in in SheetCAM (about 0.060 in real; heights also match the T45m-format chart reportedly from Jasic, see Pierce height, pierce delay, and cut height) · kerf 0.06 in · air 75 psi · THC defaults (SMART voltage, 85 % cutoff, 5 % Z-speed).

Feed rate and amps: start from the Razorweld chart’s Optimum column (CUT45 cut settings), then check them with the test-cut program’s speed ladder.

Questions I plan to ask Langmuir or Razorweld support (no answers yet)
#

  1. On a PRO with the stainless water table, where should the work clamp go now: frame, drain plug, slat bed, or workpiece? (Langmuir’s documents give different locations.)
  2. Can Razorweld confirm the chart it gave MO-Hacker (t/847/29) is for the X45 machine torch, and which arc voltages are right? Is there an official X45 machine-torch cut chart: amps, speed, kerf, pierce delay, and arc voltage by thickness?
  3. Which trigger mode (2T or 4T) should the CUT45 be in for CNC use?
  4. Can Razorweld send an electronic copy of the JRWPC45LH operating manual?

Answered or decided
#

  • Which manual applies to my cutter: my printed manual is the Razorweld JRWPC45LH operating manual (“CNC - CROSSFIRE COMPATIBLE”, unit “Razorweld CUT45 CNC”, built by Jasic). I couldn’t find an electronic copy online (October 2026). The online Razorcut 45 DI manual that earlier versions of this site cited is for a related but different model, so the pages now say so wherever they use it. See Sources.
  • Residential-supply (EMC Class A) warning: dropped. It came from the Razorcut 45 DI manual, not my JRWPC45LH manual, whose safety section has no such wording. House wiring and the 230 V circuit are still a licensed electrician’s job, and I follow my manual and Langmuir’s safety page.
  • X45 nozzle RW51206 orifice size: working answer 1.0 mm (0.040 in), per forum member mechanic416 (https://forum.langmuirsystems.com/t/21266), consistent with the torch diagram’s “20–50 amp” label. Worth confirming with Razorweld only if it matters.
  • X45 retaining cup part number (my choice): use Langmuir’s listing, RW603505T. The “60305T” on my copy of the torch diagram is treated as a typo (Air supply, air quality, and X45 consumables).
