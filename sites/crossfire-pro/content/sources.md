Every web link cited across the site, grouped by who published it. Sources are weighted as described in [About this site]({{< relref "/about.md" >}}): Langmuir first, then Razorweld/Jasic, then experienced forum members, then everyone else. Results from my own shop go in each procedure page's "Bernie's test results" section.

The Razorcut 45 DI manual and the J45XC torch diagram are Razorweld PDFs; local copies aren't included in this site. My own JRWPC45LH operating manual isn't online; pages cite my printed copy by page number.

**About the Razorweld manuals.** My cutter is the **Razorweld CUT45 CNC, model JRWPC45LH** ("CNC - CROSSFIRE COMPATIBLE", built by Jasic). Langmuir sells it as the "Razorweld Razorcut 45 with Machine Torch". The **Razorcut 45 DI** manual below is for a related but different model, even though Langmuir's Reference page links it for this cutter. Where pages use it, they say so, and its EMC Class A "not for residential supply" warning has been dropped because my own manual's safety section doesn't include it. I couldn't find the JRWPC45LH manual online (searched October 2026), so the pages cite my printed copy. Pages cite it as "my JRWPC45LH operating manual, p. N", using the page numbers printed on the booklet; each PDF page after the cover shows two printed pages side by side. The other published documents listed under Razorweld (official) (the JRWPC45LT sister-model manual, Razorweld's CUT45 spec sheet, and the JRWPC45LH torch-parts sheet) mostly agree with it. My JRWPC45LH manual comes first wherever they differ, and the DI manual is used only where mine is silent.
